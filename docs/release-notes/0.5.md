**BentoBar 0.5** (formerly DiscoBar): add, hide and organise items in your Googlebook's status bar.

### DiscoBar is now BentoBar
Like a bento box, BentoBar keeps your status bar items neatly in their compartments. It comes with a new name, a new icon and a new package (`io.github.kuscher.bentobar`). Because the package changed, BentoBar installs **next to** DiscoBar rather than over it. To move over:
1. In **DiscoBar**, open **Look › Copy settings**.
2. Install and open **BentoBar**, then **Look › Paste settings**.
3. Turn BentoBar on in **Setup**, then uninstall DiscoBar (otherwise both draw in the status bar).

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar › **Setup** › **Turn on**. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/kuscher/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **New name: BentoBar.** The GitHub repository moved to github.com/kuscher/bentobar (old links redirect).
- **New icon:** a bento box seen from above, with a status bar compartment (‹ and item dots) over the compartments for your items: rice, salmon, tamago and edamame in an ink-blue box. The themed icon, the settings header, the ‹ menu and the Quick Settings tile share the shape.
- The splash screen shows the icon on its ink background, and About notes the old name.
