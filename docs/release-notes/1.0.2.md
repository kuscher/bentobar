**BentoBar 1.0.2**: a new icon, and the Flight fix of 1.0.1.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Updating:** 1.0.1, 1.0, 0.9, 0.8, 0.7, 0.6, a 0.5 from Google Play, or a 0.5 downloaded here since 30 September 2026 updates in place, and nothing changes in your bar. A 0.5 installed from GitHub before that date carries the old signing key and Android won't update it: in BentoBar open **Look › Copy settings**, uninstall it, install this version, and **Look › Paste settings**.

**No new permission.** As since 0.9, two items go online and only after you set them up: **Weather** asks Open-Meteo about the city you search for, and **Flight** asks AirLabs about the flight you enter, with a free key of your own. Still only the flight number and your key go to AirLabs. **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/kuscher/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **A new icon.** The same bento box seen from above, the status bar on top and three compartments
  below, now in mint on deep teal. The launcher, the themed icon, the splash screen and the store
  listing all have it; the one-colour marks in the app and in Quick Settings keep their shape.
- Everything in 1.0.1: following one of the day's flights of a number that flies more than once a
  day (UA 1227: Orlando to Newark, Newark to San Francisco, San Francisco to Portland), the chip could
  stop updating and never show that your flight was late. It now reads your flight from AirLabs'
  list of the coming hours from ten hours before departure, keeps asking until it has landed, and
  checks a flight you track within those ten hours once more straight away.
