**BentoBar 1.3.1**: an open popup no longer keeps BentoBar busy.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Updating:** 1.3, 1.2, 1.0.2, 1.0.1, 1.0, 0.9, 0.8, 0.7, 0.6, a 0.5 from Google Play, or a 0.5 downloaded here since 30 September 2026 updates in place, and nothing changes in your bar. A 0.5 installed from GitHub before that date carries the old signing key and Android won't update it: in BentoBar open **Look › Copy settings**, uninstall it, install this version, and **Look › Paste settings**.

**No new permission.** As since 0.9, two items go online and only after you set them up: **Weather** asks Open-Meteo about the city you search for, and **Flight** asks AirLabs about the flight you enter, with a free key of your own. **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when. The glass's blur is drawn by the system: BentoBar never reads what is behind its popups.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/kuscher/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **An open popup no longer keeps BentoBar busy.** While a popup sat open, 1.3 drew its window again 60 times a
  second with nothing changed: about a tenth of a CPU core for as long as it stayed open. Now it draws once a second
  (for its live values, such as a timer or a song's progress) and is otherwise still.

Coming from 1.2? 1.3 brought glass popups that unfold from the bar, a shadow that sits straight, one highlight that
follows your mouse, and three new choices in Look: see the [1.3 release](https://github.com/kuscher/bentobar/releases/tag/v1.3).
