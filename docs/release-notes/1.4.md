**BentoBar 1.4**: Weather where you are ("My location"), plus smoother status-bar ticks and lower background overhead.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Updating:** 1.3.1, 1.3, 1.2, 1.0.2, 1.0.1, 1.0, 0.9, 0.8, 0.7, 0.6, a 0.5 from Google Play, or a 0.5 downloaded here since 30 September 2026 updates in place, and nothing changes in your bar. A 0.5 installed from GitHub before that date carries the old signing key and Android won't update it: in BentoBar open **Look › Copy settings**, uninstall it, install this version, and **Look › Paste settings**.

**Optional approximate location.** Weather can now use **My location**: BentoBar asks for Android's approximate location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) only when you choose it, rounds it to about 10 km (`0.1°`) before asking Open-Meteo, and keeps it in memory only. As since 0.9, only **Weather** (Open-Meteo) and **Flight** (AirLabs, with a free key of your own) go online, and only after you set them up; **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/kuscher/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **Weather: My location.** The weather where you are, instead of a city you pick. BentoBar asks for
  Android's approximate location only when you choose it, and sends Open-Meteo that location rounded
  to about 10 km. It is kept in memory only, never in your layout.
- **Smoother ticks and lower background overhead.** App shortcuts and folders now load their icons
  and labels off the main thread with bounded caches; Flight file updates run in the background;
  battery status lookups are throttled while keeping 1 Hz power readings; and item states are never
  recomputed mid-frame while drawing.
