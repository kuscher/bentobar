# Content rating (Play Console › Policy › App content › Content rating, IARC questionnaire)

Contact email for the certificate: kuscher.projects@gmail.com.

- **Category:** All other app types (utility, productivity, communication or other), not a game.
- **Users interact or exchange content:** No.
- **Shares location:** No. (The question is about sharing the user's location with other users. From 0.9 the
  Weather item sends the coordinates of a city the user picked, or with My location the device's approximate
  location, to a weather service; that is declared in Data safety, not here.)
- **Unrestricted internet or web browsing:** No. Up to 0.8 the app had no internet permission. From 0.9 it has
  one, for two fixed services (Open-Meteo, AirLabs); it is no browser, shows no web content, and opens links
  in the device's browser.

Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.
